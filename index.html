About us

We started in 2005 with one simple idea

In 2005, we opened Tapovan in Nana Varachha with a few benches, a blackboard and a lot of belief. I wanted a school where every child is known by name, and where parents can walk in and talk to us directly.

Over the years, many of our first students have come back to enrol their younger brothers and sisters. That is the best certificate we could ask for. Today Tapovan has grown into a group of campuses, but the spirit is the same: honest teaching, and discipline with kindness.

Our line, "You visualise, we realise", is a promise to every parent. Tell us what you dream for your child, and we will work hard to help get there.
